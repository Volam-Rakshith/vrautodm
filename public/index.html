Important: follow verification limitation

Instagram's supported APIs do not provide a general way to check whether an arbitrary commenter follows your account. This build can send a private reply to an eligible comment, but it cannot reliably verify a follow and then unlock a second DM. The message can invite people to follow, without claiming their follow was checked.
